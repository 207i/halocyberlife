Ocean Generator™ v2

A modular solar-thermal platform exploring controlled phase-change systems for future energy and environmental applications.

Patent Filing In Progress
Solar-thermal platform Phase-controlled systems Modular architecture Pre-release

What This Is

Ocean Generator™ v2 is an advanced exploration into modular solar-thermal architectures that leverage controlled phase transitions within sealed, environmentally isolated systems.

The platform is designed to operate at the intersection of thermal dynamics, material science, and system-level efficiency — emphasizing stability, scalability, and long-term autonomous operation.

What This Is Not

This is not a conventional desalination system, not a traditional power plant, and not a derivative of existing renewable infrastructure.

Public descriptions intentionally omit internal mechanisms while intellectual property protections are finalized.

Disclosure Notice:
Core operational principles, system internals, and performance characteristics are withheld pending patent submission and review.

Status

Ocean Generator™ v2 is currently in the intellectual property protection phase. Visuals, simulations, and detailed system descriptions will be released incrementally following patent confirmation.
