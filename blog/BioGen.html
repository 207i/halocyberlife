1. Full System Cutaway Overview

This cinematic cutaway shows both the external and internal layout of the platform, including solar collectors, optical focusing, and compact energy conversion stack.

2. Photon Concentration Process

Sunlight is captured by curved solar reflectors, funneled through fiber optics, aligned by the TRIA system, and focused into the boiler for thermal energy transfer.

3. Steam Generation Chamber

The sealed boiler receives concentrated photonic heat from the exterior, converting internal water into high-pressure steam, which rises to power the turbine.

4. Power Output System

Steam spins a compact turbine generator, sending electricity to onboard batteries and external ports. Digital controls monitor the output.

5. Water Output System

Seawater enters through an intake, is filtered and desalinated, then distributed to both the boiler and a potable water tank for additional use.
