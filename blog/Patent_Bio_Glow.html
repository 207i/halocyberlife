BIOGLOW™ — Provisional Patent Disclosure

Disclosure: This page is a public-view presentation of BIOGLOW™ materials. The original provisional filing is formatted differently for USPTO submission. This version is presented for readability, organization, and public project documentation.

BIOGLOW™ is an architectural resonance-based illumination system using integrated photonic-acoustic activation layers. The system is directed toward surface-based luminescence through a multilayer structure including a dielectric resonant substrate, conductive resonance mesh, photonic activation layer, and acoustic or ultrasonic stimulation elements.
