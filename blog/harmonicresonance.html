Three Simple Ways to Tune Yourself

1) The Humming Reset

Inhale 4 seconds. Hum out 6–8 seconds. Repeat 6 times.

2) One-Tone Focus

Play one steady tone. When your mind wanders, return to sound.

3) Coherence Breathing

Inhale 5. Exhale 5. Add a soft tone or hum.
